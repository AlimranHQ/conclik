# Conclik AI Policy

Version: 1.0
Status: Official

---

## Purpose

Conclik is an AI Gateway.

Not a single AI product.

---

## Provider Policy

Conclik shall support multiple AI providers.

No provider shall receive permanent priority.

Selection shall depend on:

- Quality
- Reliability
- Cost
- Performance
- User Need

---

## Independence

Conclik shall never depend
on one company,
one model,
or one SDK.

---

## Future

Every provider must be replaceable
without changing the core platform.
