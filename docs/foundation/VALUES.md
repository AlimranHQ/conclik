# Conclik Core Values

Version: 1.0
Status: Official

---

## Our Values

### 1. Trust

Trust is earned.

Never assumed.

---

### 2. Security

Security is not a feature.

Security is the foundation.

---

### 3. Freedom

Users are never locked
to one AI provider.

---

### 4. Excellence

Good is never enough.

We pursue excellence.

---

### 5. Innovation

We create.

We do not imitate.

---

### 6. Simplicity

Powerful systems
must remain simple to use.

---

### 7. Humanity

Technology exists
to serve people.

Never the opposite.

---

### 8. Long-Term Thinking

Every decision
must strengthen Conclik
for the next decade.
