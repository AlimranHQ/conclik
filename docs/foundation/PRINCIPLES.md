# Conclik Principles

Version: 1.0
Status: Official

---

## Principle 1

Think Long-Term.

Never sacrifice the future
for short-term success.

---

## Principle 2

Security Before Features.

A secure platform
is more valuable
than a fast platform.

---

## Principle 3

Architecture Before Implementation.

Good design
reduces future complexity.

---

## Principle 4

Every Component Must Be Replaceable.

No module
shall become irreplaceable.

---

## Principle 5

Documentation Is Part Of Engineering.

Undocumented systems
are incomplete systems.

---

## Principle 6

Every Decision Must Have A Reason.

Never change
without documented justification.

---

## Principle 7

Quality Is A Habit.

Not A Final Step.

---

## Principle 8

Conclik Exists To Serve Humanity.

Technology is the tool.

Humanity is the purpose.
