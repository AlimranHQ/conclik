# Founder Promise

Version: 1.0
Status: Official

---

I do not build Conclik
to compete.

I build Conclik
to contribute.

---

Technology without ethics
has no future.

Power without responsibility
has no value.

---

Conclik shall always protect
its users,
their creativity,
their memories,
and their trust.

---

Every line of code
must serve humanity.

Every engineering decision
must strengthen the future.

---

I can.

I will.

I will prove it.

In Sha Allah.
